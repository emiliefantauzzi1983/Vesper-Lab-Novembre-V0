Haussmann or hygge? Two ways of lighting a shop window

Back to the 2000s.

I was working in retail in France, and back then, a shop window had one job: to be seen. On Boulevard Haussmann, every window was a show. Strong lights, big staging, and the windows stayed lit all night, even when the street was empty.

At the same time in Copenhagen, it was a whole different mood. Softer light, warmer, more intimate. The kind of window that feels like an invitation to come in and feel at home.

On one side, the show. On the other, the atmosphere. And honestly, I love both.

Now that I live in Copenhagen, I see these two worlds side by side every day, so I've decided to share what I notice, episode by episode.

What's a shop window you still remember? Write to me, I'd love to hear it.
