Why a lab

Because no playbook survives contact with a new market unchanged.

What works on Strøget is not what works in Le Marais. We test on your floor, keep what performs, and adapt the standard to each market instead of copy-pasting it.

Vesper is the evening star: the first light you set your course by. That is the job here. A fixed point of craft, read under every sky.
About

Fifteen years inside the great houses.

Emilie Fantauzzi Miquel built her expertise in luxury retail on the floor of Louis Vuitton's 1,800 m² Paris flagship. At Lancel she codified the brand's visual identity in its global playbook, supported more than sixty store managers and led expansion in key markets: Europe, China, the Middle East.

Based in Copenhagen, she now brings this know-how to Scandinavian and French houses looking for support with their retail development, image and brand experience.
