Service 01

Sales ceremony & client experience

The ritual that turns a visit into a client relationship. Learned at Louis Vuitton, written for your brand, delivered by your teams.

What it covers

Excellent service, shared and embodied by every client advisor.

  • Floor observation. Days spent in your stores, watching how customers are welcomed, served and seen out, before anything is written.
  • Your ceremony, codified. Welcome, discovery, presentation, closing, seeing the client out and building loyalty, written in your brand's voice as a document your teams keep and use.
  • Manager and team coaching. Training sessions rooted in the floor, in the real situations your teams face.
  • Follow-up reviews. Return visits to see what holds, what slips and what the ritual needs next.
I

Observe

Our immersion starts in the store, as close as possible to your reality, in the energy of peak hours as in the calm of quiet ones.

II

Codify

The ceremony is written for your brand, your price point and your customer.

III

Embed

Coaching, role play and reviews until the ritual belongs to the team.
