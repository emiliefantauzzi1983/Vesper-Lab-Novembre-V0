Service 03

Concept deployment & store openings

From the architect's plans to opening day. Openings, refurbishments, rollouts and pop-ups, held together across markets.

What it covers

Opening day is a deadline that does not move.

  • Rollout planning. The retroplanning from concept sign-off to opening day: who does what, by when, with which budget.
  • Vendor and supplier coordination. Architects, production vendors, installers and local teams briefed with technical specifications and held to them.
  • On-site installation. Presence in the store during the critical days, from structural setup to team briefings to the ribbon.
  • Pop-ups and corners. Concepts for department stores and short-life spaces, from briefing to final installation.
I

Plan

Timeline, budget and responsibilities, agreed before work starts.

II

Coordinate

Vendors, teams and installations, briefed and followed through.

III

Open

On-site through the final days, so opening day opens.

Where this comes from More than fifty openings, refurbishments and rollouts delivered across Europe, China, Dubai and Moscow, plus pop-ups at Galeries Lafayette, Printemps and Harrods.

An opening date on the calendar?

The sooner the conversation, the calmer the final week. Bring the plans, we bring the method.
