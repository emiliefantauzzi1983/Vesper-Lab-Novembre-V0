Why a lab

Because no playbook survives contact with a new market unchanged.

What works on Strøget is not what works in Le Marais. We test on your floor, keep what performs, and adapt the standard to each market instead of copy-pasting it.

Vesper is the evening star: the first light you set your course by. That is the job here. A fixed point of craft, read under every sky.
About

Fifteen years inside the great houses.

Emilie Fantauzzi Miquel trained in luxury retail at Louis Vuitton, on the floor of the 1,800 m² Paris flagship. At Lancel she wrote the visual merchandising playbook adopted brand-wide, trained more than sixty store managers, and led openings and rollouts across Europe, China, the Middle East and the UK.

Based in Copenhagen, she works with brands across the Nordics and France that want their stores to carry the same standard of craft.
