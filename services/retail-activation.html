Service 04

Retail activation & brand moments

Pop-ups, launches and in-store moments that earn their space. A campaign becomes something a customer walks through, touches and buys from.

What it covers

An activation is theatre with a sales target.

  • Concept and mood board. An in-store idea that extends your campaign instead of decorating next to it, designed around the customer journey from the door to the cash desk.
  • Props and set design. Bespoke props, materials and objects that bring the campaign into the store and give customers something to look at, touch and photograph.
  • Music and atmosphere. A playlist, a sound, a scent, a light setting: everything that sets the mood from the first step inside.
  • The house's storytelling. Savoir-faire, archives, the workshop and the people behind the product, staged in the store and given to the teams so they can tell it.
  • Events and launches. Private views, client evenings and launch days designed with the store team, from guest list to follow-up.
  • A KPI on every element. Each stop in the activation carries a commercial job: traffic, average basket, units per transaction. What cannot be measured gets redesigned or cut.
